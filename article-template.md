Start with the paragraph that hooks the reader. The first letter becomes a large drop cap automatically.

## A section heading

Write normally. Use *italics* for emphasis and **bold** sparingly.

Inline math goes between single dollar signs, like $E = mc^2$.

Display math goes between double dollar signs on its own line:

$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$
